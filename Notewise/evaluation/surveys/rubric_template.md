# Notewise Student Evaluation Rubric Form

**Evaluator Role:** Classmate Tester / B.Tech Student  
**QA Lead:** Samir (Member 4)

Please rate the generated study materials on a scale of **1 to 5** for each dimension:

| Rating | Definition |
| :---: | :--- |
| **1** | Poor / Unsatisfactory — Unusable for study |
| **2** | Below Average — Noticeable omissions or inaccuracies |
| **3** | Acceptable — Covers the basics adequately |
| **4** | Good — High quality and very helpful |
| **5** | Excellent — Flawless, comprehensive, and exam-ready |

---

## Evaluation Dimensions

### 1. Coverage (`coverage_rating`)
- Did the notes and cheatsheet capture all critical concepts taught in the lecture?
- Were definitions, theorems, and formulas fully represented?

### 2. Technical Accuracy (`accuracy_rating`)
- Are the explanations, algorithmic complexities, and technical relationships correct?
- Did the LLM introduce any hallucinated or erroneous statements?

### 3. Readability & Clarity (`clarity_rating`)
- Is the text logically structured with clean bullet points and concise definitions?
- Is the mindmap hierarchy easy to navigate and visually intuitive?

### 4. Exam Usefulness (`exam_usefulness_rating`)
- How effective is the Cheatsheet for quick revision 15 minutes before an exam?
- Would this replace 1–2 hours of manual note-taking?
